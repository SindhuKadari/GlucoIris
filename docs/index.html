( 01 ) Idea title

One fundus image → a verdict you can check

One retinal fundus image is quality-checked, analysed by four trained models with visual evidence, signed by a clinician and returned as a report in the patient's language.

The problem
Screening does not scale
DR is a leading cause of preventable blindness; specialists are scarce, unusable images reach classifiers, and a bare AI score gives the clinician nothing to verify.
Our idea
Gate · explain · sign off
Block bad images first, show where the model looked (Grad-CAM, lesion boxes, vessels), keep the model output apart from the clinician's signed grade.
Objectives
① Guided capture-to-report workflow
② Block unusable images
③ Real models with visible evidence
④ Separate model output from sign-off
⑤ Obvious verdict in EN · తెలుగు · हिन्दी
⑥ Honest — nothing simulated is passed off as real

( 03 ) Feasibility, viability & challenges

Real-world ready, honestly tested

ChallengesMitigations
Poor image quality
Blur, low light, under / over-exposure, black borders, low resolution.
→
Quality gate + guided recapture
Rule-based checks on 9 dimensions; poor images sent back for recapture. 92.6% real pass · 7 / 11 defects blocked
Class imbalance
Few severe / proliferative samples can bias the model.
→
Weighted training
Class weights in the loss, class-balanced sampling, stratified data splits, augmentation.
Black-box predictions
Clinicians need the reasoning behind AI decisions.
→
Grad-CAM explainability
Heatmaps + ranked boxes show model attention; lesion boxes shown separately. Builds clinician trust.
False positives / negatives
Unnecessary referrals or missed cases.
→
High-sensitivity screen + sign-off
B4 misses only 4 of 69 DR eyes (94.2% sens); re-calibrated threshold gives 81% / 82%; a clinician signs every result.
Low connectivity
Rural camps may have no reliable internet.
→
Offline-ready architecture
Models run locally on a CPU; records stay on the device. Cloud sync when online is planned.
Domain shift & generalisation
Different cameras and regions change model behaviour.
→
External validation & field testing
Tested on 103 unseen IDRiD images (AUC 0.870); field evaluation before deployment.
